Membership FAQ

Common questions.

Can I cancel anytime? ›
Plans require a minimum 3-visit commitment. After your 3rd visit, you can pause or cancel anytime, just let us know before your next scheduled visit and we'll take care of it.
What if I need to skip a month? ›
You can pause your membership at any time. We'll put your slot on hold and pick back up whenever you're ready.
Does the membership include interior cleaning? ›
Yes. Every plan includes a full interior and exterior clean at every visit. Add-ons like clay bar treatment, ceramic waxing, and excessive pet hair removal are available for an extra $35 each.
When does billing happen? ›
Billing aligns with your service schedule. Monthly members are billed monthly, bi-monthly members every two months. You'll always receive a notification before any charge.
What if I'm not home during the service? ›
No problem. Exterior services can be completed without you present. We'll send a notification when we arrive and when we're done.
Is there a first-clean discount? ›
Yes, your first clean as a new member is discounted. It's our way of starting the relationship right.
